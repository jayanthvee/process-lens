condition's selector, so the search question can carry its own sentence. The compiler writes one for

## The work window must keep drawing

**Before.** Nothing asked Chrome to keep the bound tab painting, and a page that drew nothing
reported a locator failure ("19 element(s)", "no frame arrived") -- a sentence about a missing
element for a page that had not rendered.

**After** (`apps/extension/src/executor/visibility.ts`, `command-session.ts`, `service-worker.ts`):

- `keepDrawing` sets `Page.setWebLifecycleState=active` and `Emulation.setFocusEmulationEnabled`.
- `paintingFrames` counts `requestAnimationFrame` frames and **returns as soon as 2 have drawn**
  (N5); a page that draws nothing is counted for the 2 s limit. A page that cannot be evaluated
  answers -1, treated as unknown, never as "not drawing".
- `Page.bringToFront` is sent **only when the first count drew nothing** (N6), and the page is then
  counted again before parking.
- A step whose page still draws nothing is refused with "The ProcessLens window isn't drawing. Bring
  it to the front and press Try again." -- never "not found".

**The tests.** `apps/extension/tests/pl123-paint.test.ts` (6): the two focus commands and no raise;

## Review fixes

### B1 -- the searched text in the search box is never a result

**Before.** After a search the typed text shows twice: as a chip inside the search box and on the
result. Every ladder read the chip as a second match, so the duplicate check skipped a row that was
not there, the "did the search find it" branch never parked, and the click that opens an item could
land on the chip.

**After.** A step or condition can say `exclude_search_box: true` (schema: a step property and a
selector property). The page-side resolver then refuses the search box and anything inside it -- an
ancestor with `role=searchbox` / `role=search`, an `<input type=search>`, a `role=search` form, a
search-view wrapper class, or a facet chip. Only *ancestors* are judged, so a result container that
merely holds a search box is still a result. The compiler sets it on:

- the not-found branch's `element_absent`,
- the duplicate branch's `element_present`,
- any click whose ladder names a `{{row.*}}` value (the click that opens the found item),
- the duplicate search's `element_present`.

The runner carries the flag on the wire and on the snapshot command, and re-asks a condition's
find with it set.

**The tests.** `apps/extension/tests/pl123-search-box.test.ts` (5): a chip plus a card resolves the
card with no ambiguity (and is genuinely ambiguous without the flag); a chip alone satisfies
`element_absent` (and would not without the flag); a result container holding a search box stays a
result. `tests/runner/test_pl123_chip.py` (3), with the Events and price shapes: row 2's email only
in the chip -> the row is **added, not skipped** (and would be skipped without the flag); a chip plus
a card -> the row opens the card, not "More than one product matches".

### B2 -- no site nouns in `packages/compiler`

**Before.** The compiler had written Odoo's own vocabulary into recipes: an "open the attendees of
the event" intent, "Search the attendees for this email", "the attendee with this email address",
Odoo's "Search..." placeholder as a rung, and "Save manually" ahead of "Save".

**After.**

- No branch on any site's noun. A count button's intent is the cleaned label: "open the attendees".
- The duplicate search is named and intended from the key column: "search for the row's <column>",
  "run the search for the row's <column>", "a result with this <column>", "Skip a row whose <column>
  is already there".
- Its box is the searchbox rung the recording itself typed into; with none, `role=searchbox` alone
  plus the intent (schema: a role rung's `name` is now optional, so a box can be named by its role).
  No site's placeholder is ever written.
- Save rungs are `role=button name=Save` first, then "Save manually".
- `_is_back_click` matches "back" only as a word or a class part bounded by `_`/`-`/the edges, so
  "backorder" and "background" are not a way back (N1).
- `_edit_shape` keeps the leave when it is the last click after the change, even with earlier
  strays (N3).

**The tests.** All the PL-123 fixtures and the compiler's own, plus
`packages/compiler/tests/test_pl123_back_word.py` (4), and every affected older test updated to the
key-column wording. `test_pl123_events_owner.py` asserts the exact key-column sentences and that the
search box is `[{"by": "role", "role": "searchbox"}]` -- nothing Odoo's.
