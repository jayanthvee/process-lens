# PL-104 report: the work window can't be hidden by accident, and says what it is

Status: built and tested; one e2e owed in the combined e2e run.

## What changed

1. **Always its own window.** `bindActiveTab` no longer falls back to a tab in the person's window.
   A window Chrome returns without its tab list is asked for its tab (`tabs.query`), which is a
   likely cause of the fallback on the pilot call. With no window at all, Connect stops in `idle` and
   says: "Chrome didn't let ProcessLens open its own window. Close a few Chrome windows, then press
   Connect again." (`WORK_WINDOW_REFUSED`). Nothing is sent to the hub.
2. **It says what it is.** The window's blank first page gets a title and one paragraph ("ProcessLens
   does its work in this window. Leave it open…"), written through the debugger. The page stays
   `about:blank`, so the run's first navigate and the abandon guard's retirement are unchanged; the
   debugger is released right after, so connecting holds nothing new. A page that can't be written
   to stays blank.
3. **Show it and continue.** A run paused with `window_hidden` shows one button under the reason. It
   asks the extension to bring the work window forward (un-minimize, focus, work tab active), then
   resumes. From the website it goes through the content-script bridge, which only answers the
   ProcessLens address; from the side panel (PL-105) straight to the worker.
   The pause sentence now says "Press Show it and continue."
