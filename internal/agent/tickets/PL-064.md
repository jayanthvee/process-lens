## Why
The brief's use case 2 is candidates: create a candidate profile, never a second one for the same person
(`internal/docs/PRODUCT.md`). The full version reads résumé PDFs. This ticket proves the **create-without-duplicate**
half on a second real app, Twenty CRM, from a spreadsheet. Twenty has a reset hook and helpers already
(`tests/e2e/real_apps.py`: `login_twenty`, `twenty_list_people`, `twenty_people_with_email`) but **no golden**. GLM is
using Odoo right now, so this stays on Twenty.

## The test: `tests/e2e/test_twenty_candidate_golden.py`
Model it on `tests/e2e/test_odoo_lead_golden.py`: the same fixtures, marks, reset (`reset.py twenty`), popup form,
trace file under `.cache/pl064/`, and harness calls.
- **Demonstration:** People → search for the candidate's email → (no match) → "+ New" → First name, Last name,
  Email, Job title → pick the Company from its relation picker → the record saves.
  - Twenty saves inline as fields change: find out what the recording marks as the commit, and say in the report.
- **The sheet:** a five-row CSV, `fixtures/demo/twenty-candidates.csv`. Use fake people with `.test` emails. One row's
  email already exists in the seeded Twenty data (pick one from `seed_twenty.py`), and one row repeats another row's
  email in different case.
- **Assertions over Twenty's API:**
  - exactly one person per distinct email (lower-cased);
  - the pre-existing one is `skipped` (already there) and wasn't modified;
  - the case-duplicate row is `skipped`, or fails the run's uniqueness check, whichever the ledger does: quote it;
  - the other three are created with the right job title and company, and are `verified`.
- **Re-run:** run the same sheet a second time; nothing new is created and every row ends `skipped`.
