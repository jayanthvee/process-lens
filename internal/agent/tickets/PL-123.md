## Why (owner's price task, 2026-10-28, `tests/fixtures/owner/price_update_kanban.json`)

- **The form recorded steps the person never did.** Opening the product recorded 13 of them:
  - "fill input — on" twice;
  - Goods, Service and Combo "on";
  - Track inventory "on";
  - the old price 910.00, the cost 455.00, category "All", and reference "PL-DESK-1001".

  Replayed, these would copy one product's cost, category and reference onto every other product.
  PL-113 was meant to stop them.
- **Stray clicks while looking for the way back:** All Apps, the tab strip, Close menu. Then the
  real leave, `button.o_back_button`.
- **"The app saves by itself" compiled `end-record-unchecked`.** The leave wasn't recognised
  behind the stray clicks, so no commit and no read-back were built, and every row would park.
- **The check step** matched Sales price to `New Price` but still asked "Which column has the
  sales price amounts?".
- **The panel** said "The Click button click saves your work" after 1 step, and the name box held
  an old task's name ("New lead").
- **The owner's note:** a real employee may click the product card instead of typing in the search.
  Both must work.

## Cause of the "fake" steps (owner's hypothesis, confirmed by T1 in the code)

The recorder's state-diff capture (PL-018, built to catch autofill, paste and password managers)
compares every field before and after each action (`content/recorder.ts`, `checkpoint()`). When a
single-page app opens a form in the same document (Odoo opening a product or an event), every
field goes from absent to a value. Each is emitted as a fill with `page_set: true` and
0 keystrokes.

The compiler already drops `page_set` fills (`compiler.py`, `_is_page_set_fill`), so they never
reached a recipe. The panel ignores `page_set` and lists them as "Typed …", which misled the owner
and T1.

**So item 1 changes:**
- The panel hides `page_set` steps behind "Hidden: N values the page filled in itself. Show".
  This needs no model.
- `clean_recording` is only for stray clicks (menus opened and closed, tabs looked at, All Apps).

## Change

1. **`clean_recording` at Stop** (using PL-122's `services/ai`).
   - It takes the recorded steps and the page context and returns, per step, keep or drop, and a
     one-sentence `intent`.
   - Code gate: only drops and intents may differ from the recording. No step is added, and no
     value, target or order changes.
   - A dropped step is one the page did by itself (a field set as the form opened, with no input
     from the person before it) or a click that had no effect on the task (menus opened and
     closed, tabs looked at).
   - The panel shows the kept steps with their intent and a "Hidden: N steps the page did by
     itself. Show" line. The person can bring any of them back. Save sends the confirmed list.
   - With no model, today's list is shown unchanged.
2. **Intent in the recipe.** Each compiled step carries `intent`. Add `intent` to the recipe
   schema as an optional string, with a schema test.
3. **The leave is found after stray clicks.** For "saves by itself", the leave is the last step
   after the changed fields that takes the page off the item. That means a navigation, a click
   whose landing page differs, or a click on a control named or labelled as going back. Dropped
   steps don't count. The owner's fixture must compile to:
   - a commit on the re-open, with a `value_equals` read-back of Sales Price;
   - no `end-record-unchecked`.
4. **"Did the search find it" is an intent, not a table cell.** For a find-then-update, the
   branch's condition carries `intent: "the search result for {{row.<key>}}"`, and its targets
   don't assume a table cell. With PL-122 this finds a card or a row.
   - The click that opens the item is the same: a card or a row, found by the row's own value,
     never by position.
   - A task taught by clicking the card without searching compiles to: open the list, find the
     item by the row's value, open it.
5. **Panel.**
   - The save line appears only after Stop.
   - The name box starts empty or as "<site> task".
   - The check step shows Matched when a column is matched, with no leftover question.

## Events enrollment (owner's run a22a17be, `tests/fixtures/owner/events_enroll.json`)

What broke, and what this ticket must make right:

- **Opening the event.** `click-1` targets `role=main` named "demo dayyyyyyyMy Company", the whole
  card area with its text glued together. Try one row parked: "the click landed on a different
  element". Item 4's rule covers it: open the item by the row's own value (the event name), never a
  container or glued text.
- **The Attendees button.** `click-2` targets "0Attendees", a count glued to a label. After one
  attendee it reads "1Attendees". A target name must drop counts and numbers that change. Add the
  step's intent ("open the attendees of the event") so PL-122 can find it when the name differs.
- **The save.** The commit is a click on the **Questions tab** (`role=list`) with
  `text_count_increases`. A tab is never a save (PL-113 meant to refuse it). Odoo saved the attendee
  by itself. "The app saves by itself" must be offered and be the default here. Its read-back is
  the attendee's email on the saved record.
- **The key.** The duplicate check uses Attendee Name. When a column looks like an email, it is
  the default key.
- **Form-load fills.** The event form's own values were listed in the panel: name, both dates,
  organizer, responsible, venue, and limit registrations "on". `clean_recording` drops them, as in
  item 1.

- **Saved without leaving (found by T1 reading ahead, not yet seen in a run).**
  - The owner never left the attendee form. Odoo saved it in place: the address went from
    `.../attendees/new` to `.../attendees/1`. PL-118's saves-by-itself needs a leave, and with none
    it compiles `end-record-unchecked`, so every row would park.
  - Today's commit (the Questions tab, `text_count_increases` on the name) can't pass either: a
    name inside an input isn't page text, and a tab click saves nothing.
  - Rule: when the address moves from `.../new` to `.../<id>` during the recording, the step
    before that move is where the app saved. The check is PL-059's landing on `.../<id>`, plus a
    `value_equals` read-back of the email on that page.
  - If the move happened with no step of the person's, the app saved because its window was hidden.
    T1 reproduced this: firing `visibilitychange` to hidden made Odoo save `/attendees/new` as
    `/attendees/2` with no click. A run can't rely on that.
  - The commit is then an explicit save: the form's own save control, found by the intent "save
    the record" (Odoo's is an icon button with aria-label "Save manually"), or else the leave
    (the breadcrumb back to the list). Never a reload: reloading a `/new` page throws the entry
    away.
- **The sheet's Event column is unused.** The event is opened by the taught name. When the sheet has
  a column whose values match the event opened while teaching, open the event by that column.

Tests: the Events fixture compiles to:
1. open the events list;
2. open the event found by `{{row.event}}`;
3. open its attendees;
4. New;
5. fill name, email and phone;
6. a saves-by-itself commit with an email read-back, and the email as the key.

No step targets a glued or count-bearing name.

## Found by T1 walking the price task by hand, like a person (local Odoo 18, 2026-10-28)

Each must hold in a test:

1. **The product name shows twice after a search:** once in the search chip and once on the card.
   "Open the product found by `{{row.product}}`" must pick the result, not the search chip.
2. **Case and spaces:** searching "oak standing desk " (lower case, trailing space) finds "Oak
   Standing Desk". Matching the result to the row is trimmed and case-insensitive.
3. **A name several products share** ("Standing Desk" gives Birch, Oak and Standing Desk Power
   Bar): the row parks with "More than one product matches '<value>'." It never picks one.
4. **The back control depends on window width.**
   - In a narrow window it's an arrow button, `button.o_back_button`.
   - In a wide window it's the breadcrumb link "Products", an `a` inside an element that also
     carries `o_back_button`.
   - The owner taught narrow, and the work window may be wide. The leave step needs the intent
     "go back to the product list", and the recorded CSS alone isn't enough.
5. **The browser's Back button saves too, but the screen lags.** The address changes to
   `/odoo/products` while the form stays on screen for seconds. "Left the page" is decided by the
   list being shown, not by the address.
6. **A failed save:**
   - When Odoo refuses (e.g. a required field is empty), the page stays put and shows the toast
     "Invalid fields: Name". There is no dialog.
   - The row must park with that toast's words ("Odoo didn't save it: Invalid fields: Name"), not
     with "couldn't find" the re-open step.
7. **"$799" typed into Sales Price is accepted and saved as 799.** Sheet values with a currency
   sign are fine.
8. **"Sales Price" also appears in the chatter's change history** ("(Sales Price)"). A text-only
   rung for the field is ambiguous, so the field's role and label rungs come first.

## The work window must keep drawing (found by T1, likely the owner's black window)

In T1's hidden browser pane, Odoo's page stayed empty (an empty body, every file loaded) until a
screenshot forced the window to paint. Then it drew at once. Odoo waits for the window to paint
before it renders. Chrome on Windows stops painting a window that is covered or in the background,
so the work window can sit blank and every step finds nothing. That matches the owner's black work
window during the price run.

- Find out what keeps the bound tab drawing while its window is covered. Try CDP
  `Page.setWebLifecycleState` set to `active`, `Emulation.setFocusEmulationEnabled`, and
  `Page.bringToFront` before a step. Report which works.
- The executor reports when the page has not painted for 2 s during a step (a
  `requestAnimationFrame` counter), and the row parks with "The ProcessLens window isn't drawing.
  Bring it to the front and press Try again." It must not say "not found".
- Proof: one owner run with the work window covered by another window, recorded in the report.
