# PL-085: `assert` and `wait_for` steps work in the real extension

Owner: T2 (DeepSeek) · Reviewer: T1 · Branch `pl-085` (from `main`)
Time box: 60 minutes

## Why

`services/runner/creation.py` lists `assert` and `wait_for` as executor actions, and the recipe
schema has both. But in the real extension, every non-commit step goes through
`resolveStepTarget` in `apps/extension/src/executor/step-runner.ts`, which refuses a step with no
`targets`:

> step assert-something-to-save has no target ladder

So an `assert` or `wait_for` step parks every record. T1 hit this while reviewing PL-079. Neither
the compiler nor the goldens produce these steps yet, which is why nothing caught it. An
`assert` or `wait_for` carries its target inside its `condition`, not on the step.

## Scope

1. In the executor's step path, an `assert` or `wait_for` step resolves **no step target**. It
   evaluates its `condition` with the same `conditionExpression` that `assert_after` uses,
   polling until the condition holds or the step's `timeout_ms` (for `assert`, the condition's
   own `timeout_ms`, else the schema default) runs out.
   - `assert`: a pass is ok; a miss is a step failure with "the page did not show what this step
     checks", which parks the record as any failed step does today.
   - `wait_for`: a pass is ok; a timeout is the same kind of failure.
2. The runner's side (`services/runner/run_loop.py`): check that it sends these steps the way the
   executor now expects, and doesn't call `prepare` for them. Fix it only if it does.
3. No change to `branch` (already a runner action) or to commit steps.

## Gates

- Tests first:
  - extension tests for an `assert` that passes, one that fails after its timeout, and a
    `wait_for` that holds after a delay, all with no `targets` on the step;
  - a runner test, with the scripted executor, that a recipe with an `assert` step verifies.
- `npm --prefix apps/extension run check` and full `python -m pytest -q`, `ruff check .`.
- No e2e: Odoo and OrangeHRM are in use.
