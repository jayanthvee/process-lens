# PL-098: A parked record's half-filled form never reaches the destination

Owner: T1 · Branch `pl-098` (from `main`)

## Why

The pilot's messy lead file on Render left a half-filled lead in Odoo for a row ProcessLens had parked ("Jo Lee", empty salesperson). With PL-096, the lead golden's bad-salesperson row did the same, every run.

A standalone repro on local Odoo and Chrome 153 showed the PL-027 guard leaking every time, even on a plain half-filled lead:

- Odoo saves a dirty form on unload with `navigator.sendBeacon(".../crm.lead/web_save")`.
- Chrome sends a beacon (and a `keepalive` fetch) from the browser process. It passes `Network.setBlockedURLs`, and DevTools never reports a `requestWillBeSent` for it.
- With `sendBeacon` replaced in the page, nothing is saved.

## Change

`apps/extension/src/executor/abandon-guard.ts`: before blocking the network and leaving the page, the guard adds a CDP binding and evaluates a script in the page.

- `navigator.sendBeacon` sends nothing, answers `false`, and reports the attempt.
- A `keepalive` fetch is rejected and reported. An ordinary fetch is left to the network block.
- What was refused is reported through the binding into the guard's blocked list, so the ledger's proof is kept.
- A page that cannot be silenced is not left at all: the guard throws, as it does when the block is refused.

## Tests

- `apps/extension/tests/executor-abandon-guard.test.ts`: the call order; a beacon at unload sends nothing and is reported; a keepalive fetch is refused and an ordinary one passes; a page that cannot be silenced is not left.
- Live: `tests/e2e/test_odoo_lead_golden.py` with PL-096 merged in, on local Odoo: passed, with no abandoned lead in Odoo. Before this change the same run left "Golden Bad Salesperson" in Odoo.
