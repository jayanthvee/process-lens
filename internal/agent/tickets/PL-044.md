# PL-044: The save check counts the row's own entry, not a stray one before it

Owner: T2 (GLM Flash) · Reviewer: T1 · Size: **small, 1 part** · Branch `pl-044` (based on `pl-042`) · Worktree
`C:\Users\vasup\process-lens\.worktrees\pl-044` · **Compiler only:** `packages/compiler/`

## The bug, from the owner's recording
`packages/compiler/tests/fixtures/odoo_two_saves_owner.json` (sanitized, added in PL-042) starts with an **abandoned entry**:
- seq 1 **New**;
- seq 4 picks the option **"Probe Four Contact"**;
- seq 9 **Internal link**, which opens the contact's own page (the observation at seq 10 has url `.../res.partner/24`).

The owner then comes back and fills the real entry, from seq 11 to the **Add** at seq 24. PL-042 trims everything after seq 24.

`_infer_assertion_from_fills` in `packages/compiler/compiler.py` (PL-031/PL-037) takes the **first** templated fill or option pick among **all** steps before the save. So the save check becomes `text_count_increases: "Probe Four Contact"`, a constant from the abandoned entry. Every row would then be checked by counting that one contact, so a row whose card never appeared could still pass, or a good row could park.

## The fix
1. **Find the entry the save belongs to.** Walk back from the save, and stop at the last **page change** before it:
   - a step whose observation (or `after_url`) shows a URL path that differs from the save's page;
   - or a `navigate` step.

   Only steps **after** that point count as the entry. With no page change, the whole recording is the entry, which is today's behavior.
2. **Use only that entry** in `_infer_assertion_from_fills`, both for `first_entry` and for the `columns` check. Change nothing else in the compiler.
3. **If the entry gives nothing** (no templated fill and no option pick), return `None` exactly as today. Do **not** fall back to the earlier entry.

Read how `Candidate` carries the observation and its URL. If a candidate has no URL for its observation, it is not a page change.
