# PL-046: Record and replay a sales quotation on real Odoo

Owner: T2 (GLM Flash) · Reviewer: T1 · Size: **medium, 1 part** · Branch `pl-046` · Worktree
`C:\Users\vasup\process-lens\.worktrees\pl-046` · **Tests only:** `tests/e2e/`

## Why
Today the only real-Odoo replay test is a CRM lead (`tests/e2e/test_odoo_lead_golden.py`). The owner's goal is accurate record and replay on real apps. A sales quotation is the next real flow: a customer picked from a many2one, then an **order line** added in an editable list (a product picked, a quantity typed), then Save. Nothing tests list-row editing on Odoo yet. This ticket finds out whether it works and reports exactly where it doesn't.

## The work
1. **New `tests/e2e/test_odoo_quote_golden.py`**, modelled on `test_odoo_lead_golden.py`: the same fixtures, markers (`e2e`, `real_apps`), reset, the popup's Make recipe form, and the same harness calls.
   - **The demonstration:** Sales → Quotations → New → Customer (pick a seeded partner from the autocomplete) → Add a product → Product (pick a seeded product) → Quantity `3` → Save.
   - **Read the seed** in `infra/test-apps/seed_data.py`, and use partners and products that already exist. If none fit, create them in the test through `tests/e2e/real_apps.py` (XML-RPC), the way the lead golden creates its tag.
2. **Replay 3 rows** with a different customer, product and quantity each. Use synthetic values only.
3. **Assert through Odoo over XML-RPC** (add a helper to `real_apps.py` if needed): each row produced exactly one `sale.order` for that customer, with exactly one line of that product and quantity. All 3 rows end `verified`.
